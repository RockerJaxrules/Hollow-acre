# Drills (deliberate practice)

Small studies of the basic forms everything is built from. Draw one, critique it blind, log it, redo until it scores
7/10, then save the passing version (and its modules) in `exemplars/`. Drill the form a failing sprite depends on.

| # | Drill | Size | Passes when |
|---|-------|------|-------------|
| 1 | A lit ball (one light, top-left; crescent shadow; no pillow shading) | 32x32 | reads round at 1x, survives greyscale |
| 2 | One leaf bundle (rim, body, shade, notched edge, 4-5 colours) | 32x24 | reads as foliage, not a ball or a fruit |
| 3 | A bush from 3 bundles, outlined once, with a contact shadow | 48x40 | one mass, no bread rolls, grounded |
| 4 | An autumn broadleaf tree (bundles, trunk with flare, shadow, fallen leaves) | 96x112 | 7/10 and beats the game's tree |
| 5 | A pine tier, then a pine (tiers top to bottom, each shading the one below) | 64x96 | tiers differ, ragged hems, no shiny cone |
| 6 | A rock (top face lit, sides darker, cracks, moss line, brown at the foot) | 32x24 | reads as stone at 1x |
| 7 | A tree stump and a log | 32x24 | rings on top, bark on the side |
| 8 | A brick wall tile and a clapboard tile that tile seamlessly | 32x32 | no visible repeat in a 3x3 |
| 9 | A shingle roof patch and a window | 32x32 | crisp, 1:2 diagonals |
| 10 | A wooden crate and a barrel (LPC: strong top vs side lighting) | 32x32 | strong top/side difference, shadow |
| 11 | Grass tuft, fallen-leaf cluster, small flower (ground decoration) | 16x16 | readable, not noise |
