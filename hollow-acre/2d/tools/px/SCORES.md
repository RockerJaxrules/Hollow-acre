Owner: "it needs to be almost on par with Stardew Valley when it comes to animations and textures and models".
The pixel-critic scores each family blind; 8 means craft close to Stardew Valley's (its craft, not its look; this game
is darker). Owner (Nov 9): "judges are being too harsh allow a 6 to be the requirement": new art goes into the game
when a blind judge scores it 6+ and prefers it to the old (old kept as backup; installs are listed to the owner, who
can veto). Judges vary by about a point between rounds and may lean toward slot b, so close calls are judged in both
orders. Latest round first.
