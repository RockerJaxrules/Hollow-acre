# Exemplars

My own sprites that passed the blind critique (7/10+), each with a note on why it works.

- `crop_turnip_3.png` (ripe turnip, 7/10 under the STRICT crop judge, reference set = 8, Nov 10): spoon leaves with a
  bright midrib and a lit rim, fanned from a plum shoulder over a round pale bulb with a taproot; a full value range
  (lights near 210 lum), a soft contact shadow instead of a soil dish, darker lines where leaves overlap. Drawn in
  `crops.py` (`turnip`, `spoon`).

- `cow_kill_stand.png`, `cow_kill_lie.png` (the cow in the butchering kill, 6/10 vs 3/10, Nov 22): a height field for the volume, then four
  hide tones as clusters with wandering borders and fur tips, a dark-brown outline and a lit top-left edge; standing three-quarter
  (straight back, hips, udder, knees), lying as one barrel with a short neck and the head in profile. Drawn in `kill373/cow_stand.py`, `cow_lie.py` (kit `pxk.py`).
