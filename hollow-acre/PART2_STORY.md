# Part 2: the story and the mountain (outline, for the owner's approval)

From the owner's direction (word for word in `2d/tools/review/SEQUEL_NOTES.md`): a giant map and a long game, with
completely abandoned places to explore and a few small mining towns; you start low in an abandoned area (a few people),
and you have to get back up to the top, where you were heading: your grandmother's place. A big story, lots of
cutscenes, horror. The people are few, mostly friendly, not gentle but kind; you may run from one before you realize
they are there to help you.

What Part 1 already set down: the letter is from **R. Kessler, foreman**. Your grandmother "went up to the old workings
nine days ago, alone, and she has not come back down", the pass is closing, and the company is yours now. On the way up,
in the crash, the headlights pass a smashed gap in the guardrail: someone went over before you.

## The mystery
- The **Hollow Mining Company** (your grandmother's) worked the mountain for a hundred years. Two winters ago she
  reopened the old deep workings and the crews broke into a seam of ice and black rock no one had seen before, colder
  than anything on the mountain. The men who worked it longest came back wrong: they stopped eating, stopped feeling
  the cold, stopped talking. Then they walked off into the snow.
- The **husks** (the creature) are them: miners, starved, frozen and stretched, hunting at night. They still have human
  faces. The towns know. They bar their doors at night and don't talk about it to outsiders.
- Your grandmother went up to seal the deep workings herself. What she found up there, and what she became, is the
  story's last answer (to be settled with the owner before the last act).

## The mountain, bottom to top
A real-scale mountain, about 6 by 6 km and 1,500 m of climb, built in Unreal as one streamed world (World Partition), one
region at a time. Night is long and cold everywhere; the husks roam everywhere after dark.

1. **The Drift** (the valley floor where the chute runs out; abandoned). Your crash site; the frozen creek; and
   **grandmother's truck**, upside down in the snow under the guardrail gap, empty, her journal in the glovebox (she
   survived the fall and walked). **Camp Nine**, the company's abandoned lower camp: collapsed bunkhouses, the dead
   store, the tool shed that becomes your first base. A few people: Kessler, searching.
2. **The Ore Line** (abandoned). The old narrow-gauge ore railway up the river gorge: trestle bridges, tunnels, empty
   stations, the frozen lake, the first old workings to mine.
3. **Coldwater** (a small mining town, a handful of people). The general store and ore buyer, the garage (the
   mechanic can rebuild your car if you bring the parts and the money), the bar, the church with the missing miners'
   names on the wall, the clinic. **Caitie-Lynn Coyle**, Caitlyn's sister from the farm town, runs the bar (the running
   gag: she knows everything about you from her sister's letters). The mid-game home: mine, sell, buy, upgrade, fix.
4. **Deadwood** (abandoned). The frozen dead forest, the sawmill and logging camp, the avalanche fields.
5. **Halfway** (a few holdouts). The dam and its power station, the weather station on the ridge, the last stop below
   the pass.
6. **The Switchbacks and the Pass.** The road you were on, reopened once your car runs again: the guardrail gap, the
   crash site from above, the snowsheds, the pass.
7. **The Top.** The company town under the peak: your grandmother's house, the headframe, the offices, and the deep
   workings. The end of the journey.

## The story in acts (each act ends in a cutscene)
- **Act I: The Bottom.** You wake in the wreck (the first playable moments). The flashlight, the cold, the snow. You
  find your grandmother's truck and her journal. Camp Nine, a fire, a shed to hold. The first night: something circles
  the camp. A lantern comes through the trees with a rifle, and you run from it, through the dark, until the rifle goes
  off behind you and the husk that was on your heels drops. It is Kessler, the foreman who wrote to you: hard,
  short-spoken, kind. He tells you the road is shut till you can drive it, and what comes out at night.
- **Act II: The Ore Line and Coldwater.** Up the gorge on the old railway (the trestle chase). Coldwater: the few people
  left, the doors barred at night, the jobs, the store, the garage, Caitie-Lynn. You mine, sell, buy tools and parts.
  The company's records show what the deep crews hit. A husk at the bar's window one night.
- **Act III: Deadwood and Halfway.** The husks are more and bolder. Someone from town is taken. The sawmill. Your
  grandmother's tape recordings, left for you along the way: what the men turned into, and that she was going up to
  end it. The husk that hit your car has a face you have seen in a photo.
- **Act IV: The Top.** The car rebuilt, the drive up the switchbacks past where you went over, the pass, the company
  town, your grandmother's house, the deep workings. The ending.

## Cutscenes (first person, the player's own eyes, as the crash)
The crash (done); waking in the wreck; grandmother's truck; the lantern in the trees and the chase; Kessler; the
trestle; arriving in Coldwater; the husk at the bar window; the taken townsperson; the sawmill; each of her tapes; the
car's first start; the drive up past the gap; the pass; the house; the deep workings; the end.

## Playing it
Survival first (running, sneaking, hiding, the flashlight's battery, body heat, stamina), bases you fortify, mining in
the old workings, selling ore and buying tools and car parts, rebuilding the car, and the long climb. Co-op for up to
four (as `PART2_PLAN.md`), everything networked from the first playable piece. Every character and animation is our
own (MetaHumans for the people; animated by us, no motion capture), at the realism standard of the crash.

## Building it
Region by region, bottom to top, each playable before the next: first **Act I in The Drift** (the wake, the truck, Camp
Nine, the first night and the lantern chase), with the systems it needs (the player on foot, flashlight, cold, stamina,
crouch, the shed base, the husk hunting at night, co-op).
