**Grandpa called three days ago and told you not to come. You are driving there anyway.**

Hollow Acre is a pixel-art farming game with a horror story under it. By day you work your grandfather's farm at the end of Route 9: till, plant and water, fish, cook, forge and build, sell at the market, and get to know the people of the valley. By night, something stands in the corn. It only moves when you aren't looking at it.

### What's in it

- **A farm to bring back.** Crops through the seasons, animals, a workshop, a forge, a kitchen, fishing spots, a mine and a quarry.
- **A valley full of people.** Dozens of neighbours with their own homes, routines and stories. Make friends, give gifts, and date.
- **Making things by hand.** Every craft is a short game of its own: lay the bricks, chop the turnips, hammer the blade, carve the pumpkin.
- **Nights you have to plan for.** Your lantern runs on oil. Point it at what hunts you. The church bell tolls, and some of what you see might not have happened at all.
- **A story about the Hollow.** You learn it from the places, the people and the things you find.
- **Play together.** Friends can join you in a browser or in the desktop app. Everyone online shares one valley, or a farm code keeps it private. Each player keeps their own farm, story and save, and players can send each other short text messages.
- **Play anywhere.** Keyboard and mouse, touch on a phone or tablet, or a controller (the Steam Deck too).

### This is the demo

The game on this page is the demo: **90 minutes of real play**. Only playing counts. The clock stops on the title screen, while the game is in the background, and after 2 minutes with nothing pressed. Settings shows how much time is left. At the end you can rate the game from 1 to 5 stars and write a note to the developer. Only the developer can read it.

**The full game is free to play in your browser:** https://rockerjaxrules.github.io/Hollow-acre/hollow-acre/2d/
It is also an app for Windows, Mac, Linux and the Steam Deck: https://github.com/RockerJaxrules/Hollow-acre/releases/latest
The demo's save stays in the demo, so the full game starts a new farm.

### Content warning

Hollow Acre is a horror game for mature players. It has jump scares, dread, and dead bodies of people and animals with blood. You can butcher farm animals for meat yourself, or leave it to the butcher.

Gore has three levels, in Settings:

- **Toned down:** the same events, shown with less.
- **Full:** the default.
- **Extreme:** off unless you turn it on, and it asks you first. It shows decay, maggots and butchering in graphic pixel-art detail.

The story is about people who went missing, children among them. Nothing violent is ever shown happening to a child. There is no sexual content. Alcohol appears in the story and in the brewing.

### Made by

Everything in Hollow Acre, from the art, sound and music to the code, is made by Jaxon Charlton. Multiplayer uses the Trystero library (MIT licence).

## System notes

Paste this into the page's system requirements box, or the end of the description.

> **In the browser (this demo):** a recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, on a computer, a phone or a tablet. The first start downloads about 30 MB; after that it loads from the browser's cache. Sound needs one click or tap. Keyboard and mouse, touch, or a controller. Playing together needs an internet connection.
>
> **Desktop apps (the full game):** Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit); macOS 10.15 or newer (Apple silicon or Intel); 64-bit Linux, including the Steam Deck. About 400 MB of disk space. The apps load the newest version of the game every time they start, and keep a copy for playing offline.
