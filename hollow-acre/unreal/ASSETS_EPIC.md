# Epic assets used in Part 2

Part 2 may use Epic's MetaHuman and Quixel Megascans (owner rule). Everything else in the game is our own work.
Raw Epic assets are never resold or redistributed on their own.

| What | Where | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| The driver's MetaHuman (first-person arms and hands) | `HollowAcre/Content/HollowAcre/Driver/Built/Driver/Body` | MetaHuman Creator in Unreal 5.8, built from the "Bruce" preset by `HollowAcre/Scripts/build_driver_metahuman.py` | Only the body mesh, its baked skin material and textures are committed and used (`DriverArms` poses them at 10 and 2). The character asset, face, grooms, outfit and blueprint are regenerated by the script (needs the owner's MetaHuman sign-in) and are git-ignored. |
